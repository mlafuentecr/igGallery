# IG Gallery Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026.

IG Gallery is a Chrome extension that improves the visual presentation of Following and Followers lists on Instagram.

## Data handling

The extension uses the visible content of an Instagram page only within the user's browser to apply the gallery layout. IG Gallery does not collect, transmit, sell, or share Instagram profiles, usernames, images, credentials, cookies, browsing history, or Instagram content.

IG Gallery stores only the visual preferences selected by the user — such as enabled state, grid columns, photo size, and auto-scroll — through Chrome Sync. These preferences are used exclusively to provide the extension's features.

## Analytics and advertising

IG Gallery does not use analytics, advertising, trackers, or its own servers to process user data.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date on this page will be updated. Any material change to data-handling practices will be communicated before the new practice takes effect.

IG Gallery is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or administered by Instagram or Meta.
